Core framework of `spotcool-world`: frames, `FrameProcessor`, `Pipeline` and `ParallelPipeline` (lockstep, deterministic branch order), `PipelineWorker`, the in-process bus and `BusPublisher`, `WorkerRunner` (graceful Ctrl+C, Windows included), `SimClock`, `WallPacer` and `TickSource`, `LogObserver` and `StepTimingObserver`, `WorldSettings`, the `PlantService`, `CameraService` and `BaseTransport` bases, the unit adapter, the `run_test` harness and the `spotcool-world` CLI (`version`, `run --dry-run`).
