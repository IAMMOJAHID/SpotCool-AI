The run report proves that an agent acted, and that it saw. A new "Agent activity" section (`agent_activity`) lists the accepted priority-10 writes to zone points (`ZN-*-OCC-SENSOR`, `ZN-*-CLG-SP`) per client, from the BMS's own `bms_write` records; the slots the BMS's heartbeat watchdog released; the occupancy agreement (the share of zone-steps where an agent's `perception` and the scenario agree on occupied or empty, aligned by sim time) and its coverage of the run; and the decision-to-write lags (`write_lags`). New quote blockers: no such write on the SpotCool run (`NO_AGENT_WRITES`), any heartbeat-timeout release, and, for a run not driven by the stub, no perception (`NO_PERCEPTION`), coverage below `MIN_COVERAGE` (95 %) or agreement below `MIN_AGREEMENT` (90 %). `RecordedRun` gains `writes`, `perceptions`, `decisions` and `taken`; the watchdog's release reason is `HEARTBEAT_TIMEOUT_REASON`.
