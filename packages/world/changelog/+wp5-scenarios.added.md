Scenarios, the room calendar and scripted occupants (WP5):

- The scenario DSL (`scenarios/schema.py`): a day of zone entries (`at`, scene, people) and door, booking, complaint and clothing events, on BOPTEST's 0-based day index. The loader keeps every item's line and reads an unquoted `10:00` as text, not YAML 1.1's base-60 number. YAML aliases are not supported: each one is reported, never followed.
- `spotcool-world lint [SCENARIO ...] [--clips DIR] [--step-s S]` prints `file:line: level: message` and exits 1 on any error. Errors: unknown or missing zones and rooms, a zone without an entry at the start, times out of order or off the step grid, a point in time outside start to before end (only a `to` may be the end: the last tick is a step before it), `from` not before `to`, overlapping doors, a door without a door contact, `booked_at` after `from`. Warnings: people that do not fit the scene, overlapping bookings of a room, a voice cue that falls back to chat, a missing clip.
- `compile_scenario` gives `state_at` (all five zones in `Zone` order, `door_open` only where the point map has a door contact, clothing only while people are there), `cues_between` (window `(previous tick, tick]`, `[t, t]` on the first) and `bookings_at`. Simulation time maps to the calendar as 2026-01-01 plus the seconds (`SIM_EPOCH`, `sim_datetime`); day 199 is 2026-07-19.
- `ScenarioProcessor` pushes a `ScenarioStateFrame`, a `CalendarFrame` per room when its bookings change, and the tick's `ComplaintCueFrame`s ahead of every tick. A run or tick before the scenario's start is a fatal error, never an exception. `ComplaintCueFrame.audio` carries a voice cue's file name inside the world.
- `FramePublisher` puts chosen pipeline frames (the frames themselves) on the bus topic `WORLD_FRAMES_TOPIC` ("world") for the workers beside the pipeline.
- `transports/http/calendar.py` (extra `calendar`): `CalendarStore` and `calendar_router`, serving Graph-style `ScheduleInformation` (`?start=&end=`, default the scenario's day) and an RFC 5545 ICS feed (escaped text, a UID per booking, lines folded at 75 octets); 404 for an unknown room.
- `OccupantWorker` (extra `occupants`): posts each complaint to the agent's `/chat`, or the recording to `/voice` after checking it against `VOICE_FORMAT`, falling back to chat and saying why. 10 s timeout, two retries on connection errors and 5xx only; each result goes to the dashboard as a `complaint_cue` event, and cues still waiting at `stop()` are reported as not sent. The agent URL is checked when the worker is made. Audio is never copied, cached or logged, and the worker logs no tracebacks.
- Library: `hybrid_tuesday` (the shot list's day; South stays on the complaint clip until 15:00), `all_hands_day` and `empty_friday`, all on day 199, 07:00-19:00.
- `WorldSettings.footage_dir` (`SPOTCOOL_WORLD_FOOTAGE_DIR`) and example `08_scenario_lint.py`.
