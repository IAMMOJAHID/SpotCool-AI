The stub agent commands the zones at the close of each step (the step's `tick` event) instead of on the step's `scenario_state`, which raced the tick through the pipeline. Its people come from the step's state; its bookings and 19:00 are judged at the next step's start, the first step the commands take effect in, so a booking pre-cools from 30 minutes before it, is released at its end, and everything is released from 19:00, as the BMS takes them. Every write takes effect from the next step in a paced run whose step leaves time for the stub's round trips; at speed 0 they lag by 2-4 steps, since the world never waits for an agent. Its BACnet client waits 1 s for an answer and resends once, so an unanswered request costs about 2 s, not 12. On the RC plant, `hybrid_tuesday` saves 15.45 % (it was 16.87 %, with some writes in effect a step sooner).
