The camera system (WP4): the world's CCTV cameras and a live phone camera, served like a video recorder.

- `services/camera_service.py`: `CameraService` is now one camera's provider (`spec`, `latest()`, `run()`, `set_scene()`), and `CameraSystem` runs the cameras from `start()` to `stop()`, leaving no task behind. `create_cameras(settings)` builds the five zone cameras, plus `cam-meeting-live` when `live_camera_url` is set.
- `ZoneCamera` (extra `cameras`) shows the scenario's scene per zone, from the first source that opens: our clip `footage_dir/clips/<zone>_<scene>.mp4`, a dataset segment, or synthetic frames that draw the scenario's people count as coloured silhouettes. The scene follows sim time and the frames wall time: a new scene starts at its first frame, then loops, never time-compressed. It renders 5 frames a second in a worker thread, skipping with `grab()`, and keeps only the latest 1280x720 JPEG (quality 80). The source is logged once per scene.
- `services/datasets/`: dataset indexes (`footage_dir/datasets/<name>/index.yaml`, version 1 as written by `scripts/footage/wisenet_index.py`), validated with `yaml.safe_load` and Pydantic; paths must stay inside the dataset's folder, and segments whose file is missing are left out.
- `PhoneCamera` polls a phone (IP Webcam's `/shot.jpg`) at 2 fps with a 2 s timeout, serves only an HTTP 200 JPEG under 5 MB that decodes, keeps the last good frame, and never makes the world fail.
- `CameraSceneProcessor` sets every zone camera's scene and people count from each `ScenarioStateFrame` and pushes a `CameraSceneFrame` per camera whose scene changed (all of them on a run's first state).
- `transports/http/`: `cameras_router` (`GET /cameras` with URLs built from the request; `/cameras/{id}/snapshot.jpg` with `Cache-Control: no-store`, 404, 422, 503 before the first frame, `X-Camera-Stale: 1` from 5 s; `/cameras/{id}/mjpeg` at 5 fps at most), and `create_http_app(routers)`: one FastAPI app with the cameras, WP5's calendar, CORS for local pages and `/healthz`.
- `HttpServerWorker` serves the app with uvicorn on `http_host:http_port`; a taken port fails `run()` at once, and `stop()` ends open MJPEG streams before uvicorn exits.
- `WorldSettings.http_host` (default `0.0.0.0`) and `live_camera_url`; `httpx` joins the `cameras` extra and `uvicorn[standard]` the `calendar` extra.
- Example `07_camera_catalog.py`, the privacy test (no file writes, no frame bytes in the logs) and the phone and firewall steps in `AGENTS.md`.
