Energy and energy saved are measured over the whole of each step. The seven ElectricPower meters report their mean over the step that just ended, and every other value stays the one at the end of the step (`PlantService.step()`):

- `BoptestPlant` averages BOPTEST's stored 30-second results over the step: `BoptestClient.results` (`PUT /results`, at most 30 s in all, retries included) and the time-weighted trapezoid (`step_means`). If that call fails, the step keeps its meters' values at the end of the step and the run goes on; `PlantService.power_fallbacks` counts such steps, and `PlantProcessor` records each one as a non-fatal error and a `plant_power_fallbacks` metric.
- `RcPlant` divides each meter's energy over its 60-second sub-steps by the step.
- `EnergyMeter` adds each step's mean power times the step, from the first step; an initial state's power is never counted. The BMS's `HVAC-ENERGY` point uses it too.

Telemetry no longer misses power that spikes inside a step, such as a VAV box opening wide when occupied-standby reopens its damper; sampled only at the ends of the steps, it overstated SpotCool's saving. At the end of a run, `KpiProcessor` cross-checks each run's energy against its plant's own (`ener_tot` x 1662.66 m2) and warns when they differ by more than 0.1 %. The report shows both energies, their difference, energy saved both ways and the power fallbacks, then a verdict (`quote_blockers`): only the plants' own saving of a finished, paced BOPTEST run without the stub agent, fallbacks or dropped events, and with both runs within 0.1 % of their plants' own, may be quoted. `spotcool-world run` prints the same numbers and verdict at the end. The report's power chart draws each step's mean over that step.
