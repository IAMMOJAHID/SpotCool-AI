The assembled world and a stub agent (WP11):

- `spotcool-world run [--config FILE] [--plant rc|boptest-local|boptest-hosted] [--scenario NAME] [--speed S] [--run-id ID] [--stub-agent]` runs the whole stand-in building once, from the scenario's start to its end (`--speed 0`: as fast as possible). It prints the dashboard, event stream, camera, calendar and BMS addresses at start-up, and the run id, each plant run's energy and KPIs and the report command at the end. `--dry-run` takes the same flags.
- `runner/assembly.py`: `World(settings)` builds the pipeline (scenario, calendar and complaint publisher, the BACnet BMS around both plants, cameras, telemetry, KPIs, emitter, recorder, bus publisher), the occupants, the HTTP and dashboard servers and the stub agent on one bus; `World.run()` owns their lifecycle: the BMS and cameras around the runner, the calendar store, the pipeline's end stopping the other workers, errors published from `on_pipeline_error`, a graceful first Ctrl+C and a cancelling second one, both plants always stopped.
- `create_plant(settings, run, occupancy=...)`: the RC plant's occupant heat follows the scenario (`scenario_occupancy`); BOPTEST keeps its own schedule.
- `spotcool-world replay <run_id> [--speed S]` (`Replay`) puts a recorded run's events back on the bus at the given speed and serves the dashboard and the cameras, which show the take's scenes: no physics, no BACnet, for video takes.
- `stub_agent/` (extra `bacnet`): `StubAgentWorker`, a BACnet/IP client of the BMS on its own UDP port with ground-truth perception, labelled a stub everywhere. It writes each zone's `OCC-SENSOR` at priority 10 on a change, pre-cools booked rooms from 30 minutes before, releases its writes at 19:00 and at stop, toggles the heartbeat every 30 s, and publishes `perception`, `decision` and `explanation` events (`stub: true`).
- `run` and `replay` log to stderr at `log_level` without variable values in tracebacks.
- `world.example.yaml` (every setting with its default), example `10_full_world.py`, and the "Running the world" (with the BOPTEST checkpoint) and "The stub agent" sections in `AGENTS.md`.
