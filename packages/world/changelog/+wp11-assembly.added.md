The assembled world and a stub agent (WP11):

- `spotcool-world run [--config FILE] [--plant rc|boptest-local|boptest-hosted] [--scenario NAME] [--speed S] [--run-id ID] [--stub-agent]` runs the whole stand-in building once, from the scenario's start to its end (`--speed 0`: as fast as possible). At start-up it prints the dashboard, event stream, camera, calendar and BMS addresses (a wildcard host's LAN and loopback ones); at the end, the run id, each plant run's energy and KPIs, and the report and replay commands (with the run directory and settings file of a `--config` run). A run id whose directory already holds a record is refused before anything starts, the exit code follows how the pipeline ended, and invalid settings print `run failed: ...` instead of a traceback. `--dry-run` takes the same flags.
- `runner/assembly.py`: `World(settings)` builds the pipeline (run status, scenario, calendar and complaint publisher, the BACnet BMS around both plants, cameras, telemetry, KPIs, emitter, recorder, bus publisher), the occupants, the HTTP and dashboard servers and the stub agent on one bus; `World.run()` owns their lifecycle: the BMS and cameras around the runner, the calendar store, the pipeline's end stopping the other workers, errors published from `on_pipeline_error`, a graceful first Ctrl+C and a cancelling second one, both plants always stopped. A BMS port in use is named in the error.
- `RunStatusProcessor`: `run_status` events for the dashboard: `running` after the start, then `finished`, or `failed` with the fatal error's message or `cancelled: <reason>`.
- `create_plant(settings, run, occupancy=...)`: the RC plant's occupant heat follows the scenario (`scenario_occupancy`); BOPTEST keeps its own schedule.
- `spotcool-world replay <run_id> [--config FILE] [--speed S] [--wait-viewer SECONDS]` (`Replay`) puts a recorded run's events back on the bus at the given speed and serves the dashboard and the cameras, which show the take's scenes: no physics, no BACnet, for video takes. With `--wait-viewer` it waits for a first dashboard; the dashboard app counts its `/ws/events` clients in `app.state.viewers`.
- `stub_agent/` (extra `bacnet`): `StubAgentWorker`, a BACnet/IP client of the BMS on its own UDP port with ground-truth perception, labelled a stub everywhere. At priority 10 and on a change only, it holds an empty zone's `OCC-SENSOR` Unoccupied and `CLG-SP` at 28 degC, releases `CLG-SP` and holds Occupied while people are there or a booking is near (from 30 minutes before), releases its writes at 19:00 and at stop, toggles the heartbeat every 30 s, and publishes `perception`, `decision` and `explanation` events (`stub: true`). The setting `stub_agent` (`SPOTCOOL_WORLD_STUB_AGENT`, `--stub-agent`) turns it on; a stub run's id ends in `-stub`, and its printout and report say "(stub agent: perfect perception from the scenario script, not SpotCool's agent)".
- `run` and `replay` log to stderr at `log_level` without variable values in tracebacks.
- `world.example.yaml` (every setting with its default), example `10_full_world.py`, and the "Running the world" (with the BOPTEST checkpoint) and "The stub agent" sections in `AGENTS.md`; the documented firewall rule opens TCP 8090 as well as 8081.
